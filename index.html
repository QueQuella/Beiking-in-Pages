Background
and Motivation

Cultural heritage lives through the places, objects, and memories that shape an encounter with a city. A six-day field trip to Beijing provides the starting point for this digital travelogue. This website brings together field observations, personal travel memories, and three-dimensional documentation to place heritage within its contemporary cultural context. Each chapter invites close observation and interpretation, connecting a physical encounter with the cultural stories behind it. Through interactive exploration, this project considers how digital media can support the communication and preservation of heritage.
